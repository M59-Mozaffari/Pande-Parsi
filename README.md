
# pnd_parsi

Pand-e-Parsi (Persian Wisdom) is a beautiful and intuitive mobile application designed to bring the timeless wisdom of great Persian poets and thinkers right to your fingertips. Immerse yourself in a curated collection of poems, quotes, and life advice from legendary figures like Hafez, Rumi (Molana), Saadi, Omar Khayyam, and many more.
This app is built for anyone who appreciates Persian literature, philosophy, and seeks a daily dose of inspiration and reflection.
Features
Ever-Growing Library: Our collection of wisdom is constantly expanding! New poems and quotes are added regularly, giving you fresh content to discover.
Vast Collection: Access hundreds of handpicked poems and wise quotes (پند و اندرز) from iconic Persian literary masters.
Daily Wisdom: Get a new, inspiring quote or poem every day with our "Quote of the Day" feature.
Powerful Search: Easily find poems by keywords, themes, or specific poets.
Favorites: Bookmark your most-loved poems for quick access anytime.
Share Inspiration: Share beautiful verses directly with your friends and family via other social media and messaging apps.
Elegant & Native UI: Enjoy a user experience tailored for Persian speakers with a beautiful, native Android interface.
Works Offline: Once downloaded, read your favorite poems anytime, anywhere, without an internet connection.
